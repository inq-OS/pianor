## Plank walks, shacks and posts on crooked legs
- Nothing is straight or level: walks sag between legs, every leg and post leans its own way, roofs sag and their corners curl.
- Planks are fat, each with a pale top edge and a dark gap behind it; far off they melt into one band.
- The view itself is bent: posts and masts fan outward toward the edges, level lines bow.
- Every thing has a dark line round it; inside it, few flat tones.
- Light comes from a few warm points (a lantern, a lit window, a doorway) in an otherwise one-hue night.
- A big dark thing near the viewer leans across a corner (Scabb: a post with a hanging board).

## A thatched lookout on stilts (mi2-bg-1-061)
- The first docks tower was off, too high and rather featureless; this hut is the model.
- Low and wide: the roof is as tall as the open room under it and reaches well past the platform; the legs are short and stout, with knee braces.
- The roof is a round bell of thatch, not a pyramid: wide at the lip, rounding to a small cap with a knob. Straw runs down the slope in streaks; pale grey-beige where the light falls, tan in the middle, brown under, near black just under the lip. The lip is thick and shaggy, the straw hanging in a ragged fringe.
- The rail is open: thin balusters, a mid rail and a top rail, the sky showing through. A stair on one side, a small sign: things to look at.

## Keri lighthouse, its parts and their shares
- **A fat stone foot and a slim iron tower:** the limestone foot is a little over half the whole height and about four times as wide as the iron tower that stands on it. It narrows by a fifth to its top, is laid in courses of pale block, hooped with thin iron bands in its lower half (five or six, closer toward the ground), and has one small dark window a third of the way up.
- On the stone's top a **gallery with a thin rail** runs round the tower's foot; a bell and horns hang at the tower's foot, a red slatted day mark stands on its side.
- **The iron tower** is rust red, plain, and flares out on brackets under the **upper gallery**, which is nearly twice the tower's width and has a rail too. On it a short drum, then the **lantern**: glass in tall panes between thin bars, a second rail round it.
- **The dome** is rust brown, in ribs, on eaves a little wider than the glass, with a short capped vent.
- In the evening sun the stone goes rose and the iron dark wine; the stone keeps its courses.
- Our map before this was quite wonky: the proportions between the fat base and the steel tower were off.

## The warden's door bay
- Over the main door the wall goes up into a low, wide gable, flush with the wall: about three windows wide and a third as high, with its own little roof of the same pale blue tin running back into the big roof, a cornice across its foot, and a **red half-round window** in it. The door below is grey-blue with a window each side inside the bay. The roof is pale blue tin, not grey; the chimneys are brick.
