# Proportions

Sizes against the frame in the 1991 game, measured on its 320 x 144 rooms (our pictures are 640 x 248 fine pixels, so divide ours by 2 and compare shares, not pixels).

## Horizon and sky (rooms mi2-bg-2-028, 2-038, 0-004, 1-081, 3-083, 0-109)
- A sea horizon with things in front of it sits at 28% to 40% of the height from the top (2-038 about 28%, 0-004 about 31%, 2-028 and 1-081 about 36%).
- Only an empty view of sea and sky drops it to the middle or lower (3-083 54%, 0-109 59% of a 200-high frame).
- Rooms are 320 x 144, and scrolling rooms up to 784 wide with the same height; the verb band fills the rest of the 200-pixel screen.
