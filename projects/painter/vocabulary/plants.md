# Plants

## Grass in the 1991 game (swatches grass-1 to grass-5)
- **By day, grass is soft areas, not blades (grass-3, grass-4, grass-5):** big patches of a lit yellow-green and a darker green, joined by grain; single blades only at an edge against something dark, or in the near corner as dark spikes. 126 to 171 colours in a swatch, almost half the pixels lone grain.
- **At night (grass-1, grass-2):** long curved strokes that follow the slope, three or four blues, the hilltops smooth and lit, dark tufts only at the edges.
- Ours: `knollPicture.ts` and art-direction rule 23 make grass "broken marks of 1 to 3 pixels" everywhere. MI2 keeps such marks for edges and the foreground and lets the middle be soft grain.
- IDEA(painter): in a day scene, soft grain in the middle distance, blades only at edges and near the viewer; ask the maintainer before changing rule 23.

## Foliage (swatches foliage-1 to foliage-6)
- **Jungle (foliage-1, foliage-2):** leaves as clusters of dark-edged spikes in four or five greens over a near-black green; the far layers lighter and softer, with less contrast. Steps up to 12.5 L*, the largest of any subject: leaves are where MI2 puts its hardest contrast.
- **Far hills (foliage-3):** rounded lumps in three greens, lit on top, a dark seam between lumps.
- **Palms (foliage-4, foliage-5):** fronds as long dark spikes with a lit upper edge, sky showing between them; close to the viewer they get a dark outline.
- **Trees at night (foliage-6):** near-black blue masses with lighter blue rims on the top, only 44 colours.

## A twisted tree over a night shore (mi2-bg-0-004, its left third; a chosen reference)
- Two or three trunks from one foot among boulders, each an S-curve leaning out over the water, forking twice; no straight stretch.
- The crown is a flat, wide roof over the top third of the picture, wider than the tree is tall: leaf heaps of short strokes lit blue on top over a near-black body, sky and stars in small ragged holes near its edge.
- Trunks are the picture's darkest tones with a one-pixel pale blue rim on the moon's side, set against the palest thing in the picture (the moonlit surf).
- The foot is hidden: boulders, grass spikes, shade.
- Ours: no picture has a tree. The knoll gets one like it, overhanging from the left side (it does not actually exist on Keri): recipe in [artists-notes/trees.md](../artists-notes/trees.md).
