# Sky

## Skies in the 1991 game (swatches sky-1 to sky-6)
- **Day gradient (sky-1, sky-2):** two or three blues, lighter towards the horizon, joined by a fine scatter of the neighbouring tone (not bands with a dithered seam). 31 to 103 colours.
- **Day clouds (sky-1, sky-3):** cumulus as flat pale lumps with one blue-grey shade tone underneath and a grainy edge; wisps and streaks as long thin lenses of white over two blues, with one hard bright edge on top (sky-3). The CMI skies (d111) stay the model for curl; MI2's day clouds are flatter.
- **Night (sky-4, sky-5):** three blues; stars are single pixels, a few brighter ones as five-pixel crosses; cloud wisps pale and soft, lit from below. A band of glow lies over the horizon or a hill (sky-5).
- **Storm at dusk (sky-6):** stacked horizontal banks, dark violet-grey undersides, light tops, cyan gaps between.
- Steps 3.4 to 5.5 L* (the smallest of any subject), lone grain 11% to 29%.
