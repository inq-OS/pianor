# The room's objects in two looks (ft_1_20.AC15)

Each thing in the room that can be clicked has two looks and wakes under the pointer: the piano lid is closed and opens on hover, and the door is closed and opens on hover. The door leads to a hallway running straight on from the doorway, its floor and left wall showing. The note trainer's board stands next to the piano. The window is no button (d117), so it has one look.

What each look shows and how a look is cut: `docs/keri/scene-room.md`, section "The objects' two looks". The shield keeps the contour (AC14).
