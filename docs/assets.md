# Assets and credits

Every third-party asset is listed here with its source and licence. Original art and sound made for the project are marked as such. Nothing is added without a line in this file. Free assets are welcome (d084): CC0 first, CC BY and CC BY-SA with a credit on the credits screen, SIL OFL for fonts; never NC, ND or personal-use licences. Which packs fit and how to import one: the `use-free-art-packs` skill (`.claude/skills/use-free-art-packs/SKILL.md`).
