# ft_1_27: The teacher as a character, and Kiur's reminders

A tough, demanding, never evil teacher (one woman, her spirit from the Jo Jones story: precious time, never shames, AX-2.1) comes by boat as a deadline nears and holds a check-up; Kiur reminds the student to practise the most urgent curriculum piece. Stage 1 or 2 of the [characters and teacher programme](../architect/program-characters-teacher.md).

## Rules

- Kiur, `src/core/island/kiur.ts`, numbers in `content/world/kiur.json`: `kiurAdvice(statuses, rules)` gives the forced piece (the first in `curriculumStatuses`' urgency order that owes anything: a gap of at least `dailyFromGap` points, or no level yet), how often Kiur reminds of it, and the shoulder piece (the forced piece when its band's top is below its expected level, else the first piece whose is, AC4; never a gap that rounds to 0). `kiurSays(moment)` gives the lines for `open`, `room`, `scene`: the reminder when due (daily: not yet today; every visit above `everyVisitAboveGap`), in the room one heads-up, the next on each visit (ft_1_25.AC5); in a scene only the shoulder offer, with the piece to open. Every line carries `{piece}` and the measured gap, expected minus predicted at the deadline (or minus level before a trend), in whole points (AX-2.1). The caller keeps whether Kiur reminded today. AX-3 provisional: a piece under the minimum of plays is reminded once a day, naming its plays; a gap of 0.4 rounds to none.
- The teacher, `src/core/island/teacher.ts`: `checkupsDue(day, curriculum, statuses, world)` gives the pieces she checks today, most urgent first (due today, or asked for in a `test` calendar entry; one a day each); `checkupPlan` is the turn plan (one judged student turn over the whole piece, both hands, with the staff, Wait mode); `arrivalLine` her first line (days to the deadline and the piece); `checkupVerdict(play, expected)` passes when the play score reaches the expected level, else not yet, with two lines (the score against the expected level, then the bars with a mistake: numbers up to four, else their count); `recordCheckup` keeps the visit in the world document (A.8 `teacherVisits`); `askForTest` adds the `test` entry that lands the boat today (ft_1_25.AC4), curriculum pieces only; `passedPieces` are the pieces she passed, which Kiur no longer reminds of and she does not check again. Lines `teacherSays.*` (cast-and-phrases.md section 5). `npx tsx tools/presence/simulate-cli.ts` prints what Kiur says each day and ends the deadline day with her check-up.
- In the room, `src/core/island/roomTalk.ts`: `roomTalk` gives what is said on a visit, Kiur's lines (`kiurSays`, occasion `open` on the first visit after the player was chosen, `room` after) and, when she lands today, the teacher's first line and piece; `checkupPlay` the first whole play after the check-up began (its score and the bars with a mistake, numbered from 1), `holdCheckup` her verdict kept in the world. The room (`ProfileDashboardScene`, `room/roomTalkView.ts`) says each line over its speaker's head as the conversations do, a press for the next; her first line read to the end opens the piano with her piece (`PracticeData.checkup`: Wait mode at the written tempo, no section, mode or tempo buttons; Back returns to the room, where she still waits); after one whole pass the room hears the play from the log and she gives her verdict. Today is the player's local day (the `now` switch sets it).
- AX-3 provisional: Kiur's heads-ups in the room are the teacher's boat only (a character's waits until presence decides who stands in the scenes, WP-11, so Kiur never sends the student to an empty place); her boat no longer comes for the deadline of a piece she passed; whether Kiur reminded today is kept in memory while the game is open (`room/kiurDay.ts`), so a game opened again the same day reminds once more (AX-1); the teacher stands in the doorway as a placeholder box until her look is painted.
- The boat's arrival, `content/scripts/teacher-arrives.json` on WP-07's interpreter in the southern harbour (`port`, the southern docks, ft_1_19.AC10): Kiur on the quay says `script.teacher.boatComing` while her galleon is far out, the galleon sails in behind the quay to the second berth, she steps onto the quay and walks off toward the island, and the picture fades out; Esc skips it. The galleon is the harbour picture's own boat (for now the galleon is what the teacher uses), lit for the hour and laid under the quay (`SceneLife.figures`, the layer marked `cover`; `content/actors/galleon.json` stands it by its middle at the water, checked in `pictureFigures.test.ts`); it is not moored while she is away (`PORT_MOORED`). The teacher (`content/actors/teacher.json`, the only teacher) is a labelled box until the painter's figure exists. The scene opens with `{ script: 'teacher-arrives' }`; nothing starts it yet (the map's boat and the room's check-up are the integration tag below). AX-3 provisional: the places (far sea, berth, gangway, the way off toward the island), the galleon turned bow to the island as it comes in; Kiur's line is the draft of [cast-and-phrases.md](../architect/cast-and-phrases.md) section 5.
- AX-3 provisional: the check-up is one play in Wait mode (the test's own rules, plan 4.7, come later); passed means the score reaches the expected level, no rounding up (AX-1); "I will come back" is kept by the student asking for the test (a not-yet piece stays forced); her `checkup` conversation effect is unused: the scene starts the check-up from `checkupsDue`.

## Acceptance criteria

All AX-3 provisional until built and tried; numbers are content, not code.

- **ft_1_27.AC1** The teacher is an actor (ft_1_24) with her own look; only one exists.
- **ft_1_27.AC2** Her boat is on the map, nearer as the next deadline nears (ft_1_25.AC4); she lands at the southern docks (the southern port; [keri.md](../keri.md) section 5), not at the north jetty; on landing a script (ft_1_24.AC6) brings her to the room, where she holds a check-up: the student plays the curriculum piece once, and she says the measured result against the expected level in one or two short lines.
- **ft_1_27.AC3** Kiur reminds on opening the game and in the room, more often as the delta grows (AX-3 provisional: none at delta 0, once a day at 1 to 5, every visit above 5); every reminder names the piece and the gap.
- **ft_1_27.AC4** When the top of the band is below the expected level, Kiur flies to the student's shoulder in any scene and offers one press to the piano with that piece open; Back is always there.
- **ft_1_27.AC5** Locks (later, the teacher's switch, d132 and plan 4.8): each lock says what unlocks it; the curriculum piece, its practice and the room never lock.
