# ft_1_18: Keri island: the game's world

The game takes place on Keri, a small island off Estonia's north coast with a mid 19th century lighthouse, run by volunteer wardens. A real place with its own lore gives the student a world to come back to (AX-2). Index: [FEATURES.md](FEATURES.md). Sources and reference pictures: [docs/keri.md](../keri.md). Notes per place: [map](../keri/map.md), [the room](../keri/scene-room.md), [the knoll](../keri/scene-knoll.md).

## Rules

1. **Axioms.** AX-0: the island is a reason to come to the piano; nothing on the map stands between the student and the staff for longer than one tap. AX-2: the map and the room are attraction and give no reward of their own.
2. **Method.** The maintainer supplies the facts (a map, satellite images, photos with their viewpoint). The agent documents them in `docs/keri.md` and never invents the island's layout, the buildings' places, the coastline or the warden's room; what is not given stays `KERI-TODO`. History may come from public sources, cited.
3. **Geography.** The sun follows Keri's real position (59.6987 N, 25.0227 E, the lighthouse; the sky follows it, ft_1_14). Art is tied to geography by the world model (d108). Weather from Keri's station belongs with ft_Y_14; the game runs offline (d068).
4. **Docs:** concept, method and sources are this file and `docs/keri.md`.
5. **Map of Keri:** one painted picture (d111) reached through the room's door; the warden's house and Back return to the room. Each scene of the world model has a painted place and a button when the scene is open (ft_1_19.AC2). The mark on the northern shore is a place in the world model.
6. **Warden's living room:** the player room redrawn; the same objects, each doing what it did.
7. **The strip under the room** (33 cell rows) is free for the point-and-click interface's verbs and inventory, designed elsewhere (ft_1_20).
8. **Roof and wall colours** on the map are read from drone and ground photographs and may be wrong: `KERI-TODO`: the maintainer's word for each building not clear on a photo.
9. **Private material:** the reference photographs are private; whether a picture traced from one is committed or kept private (d107) is the maintainer's choice per photo.
