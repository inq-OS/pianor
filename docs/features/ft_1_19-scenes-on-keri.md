# ft_1_19: Scenes on Keri

The student opens the map of Keri and goes to a place; each place is one painted picture. Decisions: [d112](../decisions/game.md#d112), [d113](../decisions/game.md#d113). Index: [FEATURES.md](FEATURES.md). Notes on the map and each scene: [docs/keri/map.md](../keri/map.md), [scene-room.md](../keri/scene-room.md), [scene-knoll.md](../keri/scene-knoll.md), [scene-docks.md](../keri/scene-docks.md), [scene-crossroads.md](../keri/scene-crossroads.md), [scene-port.md](../keri/scene-port.md), [scene-terrace.md](../keri/scene-terrace.md).

## Rules

1. A scene is open to every player once it is built; levels open and lock nothing ([d113](../decisions/game.md#d113)). A scene marked `closed` in `content/world/keri.json` stays closed to every player but a tester (`openScenes`) until the maintainer opens it: a scene opens only once the maintainer confirms it. Open now: the knoll and the crossroads (the road fork).
2. A character challenges the student in its scene ([d129](../decisions/game.md#d129), [ft_1_21](ft_1_21-challenges-with-characters.md)); the challenge is drawn over the picture. A character is met only after enough practice and not every day, and pressing it opens a conversation first ([d132](../decisions/game.md#d132), [ft_1_22](ft_1_22-conversations-and-phrase-book.md)).
3. AX-0: a scene is the way to a piece, never a place to stay: one press starts a character's game, and nothing in a scene must be solved before the student may play. AX-2: a scene gives nothing of its own; a close-up's job (ft_1_38, d302) is playing, and the island's lasting change shows only what was played. AX-3: what is not yet decided is built simply and marked provisional.
4. The room's screensaver visits the open scenes ([ft_1_17](ft_1_17-a-walk.md)).
5. The tester: the player named `tester` (case aside) has every scene open, a closed one included (`TESTER_NAMES`, `isTester` in `src/core/world/openScenes.ts`); the map, the scenes and the island visit read it; nothing is saved.
6. Scenes built, each one pixel-art picture per sky hour made at build time, in Keri's shared palette (`keri/keriPalette.ts`, d111):
   - **Knoll**: `knollPicture.ts`, `src/core/layout/knoll.ts`; how it is made: [scene-knoll.md](../keri/scene-knoll.md). Clouds are one stroke each, no two alike (`keri/curlCloud.ts`, d111). The right side is open to the sea. A gust runs through the grass, stems and sea in eight looks (faster in more wind), clouds drift behind the stems, smoke is one thin thread bent east (`paint/motion.ts`, `paint/smoke.ts`, `windMotion.ts`). The ground stands still (`GROUND_MOVES`; sky and sea move); sea looks and lights dissolve one into the next (`fadeWeights`); clouds glide on screen pixels. No wind-dependent white caps yet.
   - **Docks** (closed): the jetty on the north shore behind the warden's house; `docksPicture.ts`, `src/core/layout/docks.ts`, `content/scenes/docks.json` on `src/game/adventure/AdventureScene.ts`; notes [scene-docks.md](../keri/scene-docks.md).
   - **Crossroads** (open): the path's Y fork by the green hut, the lighthouse at the end of its right arm; `crossroadsPicture.ts`, `src/core/layout/crossroads.ts`; notes [scene-crossroads.md](../keri/scene-crossroads.md).
   - **Harbour** (closed): the southern port at the slipway, the wider stone dock most visitors land at; `portPicture.ts` (the surface, the underwater view and the boats, `portBoats.ts`), `src/core/layout/port.ts`, `content/scenes/port.json`; the second view is `SceneLife.dive` (`sceneLife.ts`); notes [scene-port.md](../keri/scene-port.md).
   - **Terrace** (closed): the deck before the main door of the warden's house, seen from its end by the door looking away from the house, a little south of west, out over the beach to the sea; the first of three scenes of the house. It has more on it than the real one and reaches out over the beach as a walk of boards on piles to a head over the water, where a small thatched watchtower with a fixed long spyglass stands; its rails are stout wood (a deck with features and character, no boat; wooden handrails; a small watchtower of about two floors with a fixed long spyglass to scan the horizon; torches at night, as the terrace is like the village plaza: seven torches round the deck and down the walk burn from dusk on; the other things on it are the painter's, for the maintainer to judge); `terracePicture.ts`, `src/core/layout/terrace.ts`, `content/scenes/terrace.json`; notes [scene-terrace.md](../keri/scene-terrace.md).
7. The map paints a place for each scene (the knoll a ring of stones, the docks a jetty of boards, the crossroads a Y of pale slabs; the terrace is the deck the map already draws before the house). Tests: `src/core/layout/keriMap.test.ts`, `src/game/ui/pixel/keri/mapPicture.test.ts`, `tests/e2e/room.spec.ts`, `tools/art/pictureRules.test.ts`.

## Acceptance criteria

AC2 to AC10 are AX-3 provisional (d112, d113).

- **ft_1_19.AC1** Each scene is a real place on Keri named by the maintainer, drawn as one painted picture ([d111](../decisions/art.md#d111)): seen from inside the place, lit by the hour, never darkened at its corners by a laid-on shadow (a corner is closed by placed things or left open, d111), a strip free under it.
- **ft_1_19.AC2** The map of Keri shows a painted place for every scene of the world model, where the maintainer marked it. A scene is open when it is not marked `closed` (levels open nothing). Pressing an open place opens the scene; Back returns to the map. A closed scene is painted but is no button.
- **ft_1_19.AC5** A scene shows the sky phase of the hour ([ft_1_14](ft_1_14-sky-follows-the-day.md)) and the one wind of all scenery (AC12).
- **ft_1_19.AC7** [human] The maintainer and a student visit the scenes on the laptop and say whether each feels like its place.
- **ft_1_19.AC9** Scene 1 is the grassy knoll with the bonfire place: a ring of stones with embers that glow and smoke that drifts with the wind, grass that is blades all over on ground that swells, the view south-south-west over the hill to a wide sea, the land carrying on to the left and rocky shore right-back, painted as pixel art (d111; flat tones for small things, whole runs of small steps for what shades off, point 19) in the colours of the hour from Keri's shared palette (d111), the left corners closed by placed things (dry stems, a stone among dog roses), the right side open to the sea, and a strip free below.
- **ft_1_19.AC10** The southern harbour, where most visitors land: a big, heavy stone quay seen side-on from the west, nothing in view but the sky, the sea, the quay and the boats the visitors came in, moored behind it, painted in the 1991 game's manner. It has two views: on top the quay over opaque water; a press on the water pans the view down through the surface to the rich underwater world (the quay's arches, whose tops show above the water too, a reef, fish, and things no real sea has, a locked treasure chest among them), and a press near its top or Back pans up again; Back on top returns to the map.
- **ft_1_19.AC11** What is organic in a painted place moves live, not in painted loops (the changing frames of shimmering water were too noticeable, and fish should behave like the clouds do; [d156](../decisions/art.md#d156)): the water's strokes, glints, rings and surf are lit by the game from a field the painter records; fish, jellies and bubbles swim on their own courses and come and go off the edges; the stove's flames lick, going round one smooth loop of drawings that blend one into the next at a pace that wanders (it should lick, not bite), and a fire's light and every lamp's wander in strength; the map's beam sweeps at any angle. All of it is worked out from the time and the day's seed, so the same moment always looks the same, and with `?motion=still` it stands at its first moment.
- **ft_1_19.AC12** One wind crosses each place (birds, fish and perhaps wind; d156): its gusts travel across the picture the wind's way, and the clouds, the looks, the water, the smoke, the sparks and the gulls read it, so they lean together as a gust passes. Gulls fly and the harbour's school swims as flocks (each keeps clear of its neighbours, goes their way and stays near them); a fire throws sparks that rise nearly straight, cool and lean only gently downwind, as sharp dots with no glow (the bonfire sparks are sharp dots, not glowing), fireflies show over the grass from dusk to dawn as small lights that glow over the pixels (modern-looking sparks and fireflies over 1990s pixel art are an enchanting combination), and spray flies off the rocks only in a gust strong enough. The knoll's fire smokes by day and in the half light, not at night, and the harbour's boats do not smoke.
- **ft_1_19.AC13** Painted things the wind or the water moves are painted once and moved by the game, not painted in a loop of looks (the painter's own proposals are built as well; [d156](../decisions/art.md#d156)): a moored boat rides the swell smoothly, each in its own time; its pennant and washing are cut out with their contour and fly on the place's wind, harder as a gust reaches them; the deep view's kelp sways in the current, and what changes its place from look to look down there (the fans, the anemones, the wonders) steps whole over looks that blend only the water's light, so nothing stands twice, half see-through; a fish jumps now and then in the harbour's open water and leaves a ring in the water's own pale tone; moths circle a lamp while it is lit. The map of [ft_1_18](ft_1_18-keri-island.md) reads the same wind for its clouds and sea and has its own flock of gulls. With `?motion=still` each stands as at its first moment.
- **ft_1_19.AC14** Every scene says what lives in it, kind by kind: fire, water, wind, birds and small life, each a sentence or `none:` and why (`src/game/adventure/sceneLiving.ts`; the small details and nuances of one scene must carry into later scenes). A scene in `content/scenes/` with no list, or a list with an answer missing, fails the tests; the recipes and the verdicts are in `projects/painter/artists-notes/living-things.md`, and the `paint-a-scene` skill asks the five questions before a pass is shown.
