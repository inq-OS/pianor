# ft_1_22: Conversations with characters, the phrases they teach and the phrase book

Pressing a character in a scene opens a short conversation in the manner of the classic adventure games; the character offers to teach a phrase of its style, and learned phrases fill the student's phrase book. The tug of ft_1_21 is one outcome. Releases R3 (conversation, tug, two or three visitors) and R4 (teaching, phrase book, knowledge layer). Decision: [d132](../decisions/game.md#d132).

## Rules

- AX-0: every character ties to music and every branch leads to playing within a few presses; the knowledge layer opens only after playing. AX-0.1: a taught phrase is shown on the staff and heard; lit keys are a help with a cost. AX-1: a phrase counts as learned only when played clean again on a later day; three failed tries are no loss. AX-2.1: lines are short and say what happened.
- First cast proposed by the architect (the maintainer chooses two or three and the names): the kratt, a border guard on a coffee break, a University of Tartu student checking instruments, the ghost of a 19th-century warden; spares: the näkk, the snake king; the knoll's bonfire as a voice with no body. The architect drafts the conversation text, the maintainer reads the Estonian.
- Dialogue engine: `startTalk`, `continueTalk`, `conditionHolds`, `lintConversation` in `src/core/dialogue/talk.ts`. A step gives the line, the choices that show, the world effects (ft_1_25) and the outcome (`teach`, `tug`, `checkup`, `leave`). A unit test lints every file in `content/conversations/`, keys against the strings table. AX-3 provisional, inside contract A.6:
  - an outcome ends the talk; a line after teaching or a tug is a node at the start of the next talk behind a condition, or the scene's own line;
  - a node has choices or a `next`, never both, and at least one choice has no condition;
  - a press is one step; a branch that returns to a node it passed ends there; every node must reach playing or leaving by a way no condition can hide.
- Look (painter, MI2's manner in the band's warm colours): the speaker's line floats over their head in their own colour (`talkColour`: student white, Kiur gold, each character from `CHARACTER_INK`), ink outline, no box; at most two rows a page, longer lines in pages at sentence ends (`spokenPages`). The student has no figure in the scene yet: their chosen line rises from the band at the picture's foot (`studentHead`); the figure is drawn (a student on the template, `content/actors/student.json`, d154). While choosing, the band's cells give way to a dark board (`talkBoard`) with choices, each after a quaver, honey at rest and cream when pointed at (`choiceNote`, `choiceWords`). Pictures in `src/game/ui/pixel/talkArt.ts`, places in `src/core/layout/talk.ts` (`lineSpot` keeps a line off other actors' heads; `choiceLayout`: at most four rows, `overflow` otherwise). AX-3 provisional: widths, rows and colours.
- The view: `src/game/adventure/talkView.ts` draws `src/core/dialogue/talkFlow.ts` (a line a page at a time, a press turns it; the choices on the board over the band, lit by the pointer or the arrows, picked by a click, Enter or a number; the chosen line said by the student; Esc leaves). The flag and item effects go into the `world` save document (`talkWorld.ts`, `ProfileService.saveWorld`); pressing a character meets it (`meetCharacter`). Until presence decides who is here, the characters stand for the tester profile and with `?actors=1`.
- Teaching (AC3): the conversation's `teach` outcome opens `src/game/adventure/teachView.ts` over the scene on the named piece (`core/exchange/teach.ts`: `startTeach`, `advanceTeach`, `teachAfter`, `teachLevelOf`). The piece's staff covers the lower half of the picture (the character and its line stay seen above it); the character plays the piece (its figure playing), then the student plays it from the staff in Wait mode, the notes marked as they are played; the fact (`tug.fact`, notes right first try) is said over the character's head. A try passes at the share of the level the character plays the piece at (ft_1_21.AC5); before every try the character plays it again; one passed try makes it *heard* (`MetRecord.taught`, so the conversation's `taught` condition holds), three missed end it not yet, with nothing lost. The closing lines are the character's `talk.<id>.teachHeard` and `teachNotYet`. Esc leaves with nothing kept. With learning on, teaching is learning the piece bar by bar instead ([ft_1_32](ft_1_32-learn-a-piece-bar-by-bar.md) rule 16, AC15); the three tries stay for `?learn=0` and the self-playing demo. AX-3 provisional: Wait mode at every level (learning is reading, d135), the staff's place, no try counter. Tests: `src/core/exchange/teach.test.ts`, `tests/e2e/teach.spec.ts`.
- The phrase book in the core (AC5): `src/core/exchange/phraseBook.ts` (`phraseBook`) lists every taught phrase by its character, *heard* until played clean on a later day than it was taught, then *mine*; derived, not stored (d092). The day is kept by `teachAfter` in `MetRecord.taughtOn`; the clean plays' days come from the practice log (`cleanPlayDays` in `core/curriculum/fromLog.ts`: whole plays, every note right first try, no wrong key). `taughtOn` is a new optional field of the `world` document (version 1 kept, d069.3), scaffolded on request for the architect to review (TODO below); a phrase saved before it counts from the character's `lastDay` (AX-1: no clean play from before its teaching counts). The room's book is to come. Tests: `src/core/exchange/phraseBook.test.ts`, `teach.test.ts`, `fromLog.test.ts`.
- Learn before show (cast-and-phrases.md section 5): Puuvaim's menu offers the tug only once he has taught his phrase (`taught: puuvaim-1`); before that it offers teaching and leaving. The first talk after the teaching says his taught line and leaves (flag `puuvaim-taught-said`); later talks open on the menu with the tug. The tug's own gate (ft_1_21.AC13) still asks for the phrase practised. The challenge demo scenario plays it in that order: taught, a later talk, the tug. Tests: `src/core/dialogue/talk.test.ts`, `tests/e2e/talk.spec.ts`, `tests/e2e/autoplay.spec.ts`.
- Puuvaim: `content/characters/puuvaim.json`; conversation `content/conversations/puuvaim.json` (greeting once, then menu: teach, music tie and back, leave; after teaching it sends the student to practise); three original draft pieces `puuvaim-1` to `puuvaim-3` (`difficulty` 1 to 3, d138), 4 bars each at curriculum levels 1, 3 and 5. His face is an old burl on the knoll tree (take B): he is only there when available, otherwise just a tree. Actor `content/actors/puuvaim.json` (a `sheet` actor on place `in-the-tree` in `content/scenes/knoll.json`), `puuvaimFrames` in `src/game/ui/pixel/keri/puuvaim.ts`, lit by hour through `LIT_SHEETS`; states idle, talk, listen, play, asleep. A test holds that only sky, trunk and roses lie under him.

## Acceptance criteria

All AX-3 provisional (d132) until it has been playtested.

- **ft_1_22.AC1** Pressing a present character opens a conversation drawn over the scene: the character's line and at most three choices, each one or two short sentences, through string keys. The choices include asking for a challenge (ft_1_21) once the character has taught its phrase, and leaving.
- **ft_1_22.AC2** Every character's content (`content/characters/<id>.json`, planned, `schemaVersion` 1, zod) names its kind (spirit or nature, visitor, ghost), its music tie (a style, an instrument, a memory of playing), its lines, the phrases it teaches by level and its item. Adding a character needs no engine change.
- **ft_1_22.AC3** A character may offer to teach a phrase of its style at the student's level: it plays it (sound, staff, the character animating); the student has three tries; a try is judged as a challenge exchange (ft_1_21.AC5). Played within three tries, the phrase goes into the phrase book as *heard*; otherwise the character says to practise and comes back later (the practice-based rule of ft_1_21).
- **ft_1_22.AC4** Lit keys on the EZ-220 (key lights, ft_Y_06) may show a taught phrase only as a help with a cost (the hint model of the results), never at the higher levels, and never instead of the staff.
- **ft_1_22.AC5** The phrase book lists every phrase by character and style; a phrase can be practised in the room like a short piece. A *heard* phrase becomes *mine* when played clean in the room on a later day than it was taught. A character met again checks one of its phrases from memory before it teaches the next or extends it.
- **ft_1_22.AC6** Every phrase is original or public domain, with `source` and `license`, made like a piece (ABC and meta, `piece:lint`, `status: "draft"` until a person approves it); never a real band's riff.
- **ft_1_22.AC7** The knowledge layer is off unless the parent switches it on for that player under admin. When on, a character tells about its field (Estonian folk belief, history, science) only after the student has played for it in this visit, a step at a time; its field reaches music. Control questions with two or three answers give a level per subject (0 means explained in the simplest words).
- **ft_1_22.AC8** The student's style is what fills most of their phrase book, shown in the room; there is no class pick.
- **ft_1_22.AC9** [human] A playtest with the first characters shows whether the player reads the lines and wants to come back.
- **ft_1_22.AC10** A character the student has not got to know shows as "???"; talking and leaving is not enough: only after the student picks "Who are you?" and the character says who they are does the name show, from then on and on later visits, and the question is no longer offered.
