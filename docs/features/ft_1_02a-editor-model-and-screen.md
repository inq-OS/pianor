# ft_1_02a: Editor model and screen

A person writes a piece by clicking on the staff, hears it, tries it and saves it; it is playable at once and earns XP. First part of [ft_1_02](ft_1_02-piece-editor.md) (items 1 to 3, the file-free half of 4, and 5). Preliminary (d035). Index: [FEATURES.md](FEATURES.md).

## Rules

1. **Model** (`src/core/editor/model.ts`, pure): one or two hands, a meter, bars, notes per hand (white keys; whole, half, quarter, eighth). Place, change pitch, lengthen, shorten, set a length, delete; undo and redo (`history.ts`, 200 steps). Meter changes only while empty; bars go in steps of 4, never below 4, a new piece has 8. Empty time becomes rests (`restsFor`). A note never crosses a bar line or runs into another note of its hand; the edit is refused with a reason the screen puts into words.
2. **Chords:** notes of one hand with the same start and length may differ in pitch (named by hand, bar, start and pitch). The same pitch twice is refused (`duplicate`), any other overlap `no-room`. A chord shares one length. ABC writes `[CEG]2`. Not built: different lengths on one start, rests inside chords, finger numbers.
3. **Hit testing** (`hitTest.ts`, on the layout the staff draws): note heads first (a little larger than drawn), then system, staff, bar, eighth and staff position, ledger lines included; `pointOf` is the inverse. In a bar laid out by columns a click takes the nearest column. `placeClick` decides select or add. `targetOf` names what an action at a point changes; `targetBox` (`staff.ts`) is the box shown.
4. **Writing in order** (d078): `slotsFor` gives the places for a note, shown as shadow notes; the Notes / Rests switch (key 0) writes rests (`placeRest`, `removeRest`, an optional `rests` list beside `notes`); only placed rests are drawn while writing. `staff.ts` lays out the steady staff: bars as wide as their columns, size and lines per page from the screen and the piece only (`editorFrame`).
5. **Output:** ABC plus a meta file (`L:1/8`, both voices, `Z` for a resting bar; `license: "NaN"`, `status: "approved"`, `unlockLevel: 1`, `generator.kind: "editor"`) and the chart the editor draws (`abc.ts`, `chart.ts`), proven by a property test to equal what the compiler makes of the ABC. The same `checkPiece` as `piece:lint` shows "Fits levels ..." or the first problem (string key `editor.problem.<rule>`).
6. **Own pieces** (`src/game/editor/OwnPieces.ts`): kept on this device (`pianor/v1/own-piece/<id>`, format version 1), id `own-<title>`, `source` "written in Pianor by <player>", version up on every save. In the menu for every player, open from level 1, earning XP (no limits). "Make" lists them and a "+ New piece" card; they open again in the editor.
7. **Screen** (`src/game/EditorScene.ts`, `?editor=0` hides it):
   - Pointer: a faint preview note with its name; click on an empty place puts a note of the current length; click on an empty pitch at a note's start adds a chord note; click on a note selects it, makes it longer, Ctrl+click shorter, the wheel either; Delete removes; a right click opens the bubble (ft_1_20), whose Delete removes a note and whose rest option takes a placed rest away; keys 1, 2, 4, 8 choose the length; Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Y undo and redo. Editor keys are read before the computer-keyboard piano. A new note is not selected, so a length key sets the next note's length. On-screen buttons do the same for a tablet.
   - Always the grand staff, even while a hand has no notes; a one-staff piece is saved and played as one hand; an own piece opens with all its notes. Note names on. The title is typed in a field.
   - Layout: no top bar of its own; under the toolbar two option rows (meter, bars, title, Listen, Try it; then tempo, sharp, flat and natural tools, key, Notes or Rests, pages), the staff, and a bottom bar with the lengths, delete, undo, redo and the checker's message. At 320x180 the grand staff gets 90 rows and draws at 3 pixels between lines.
   - Length buttons are the notes themselves, and rests once Notes / Rests is pressed (`lengthArt.ts`); names are tooltips. Now there is no start screen: a new piece opens on its staff in 4/4 and C major; a press on the clef or key signature opens the circle of fifths, whose tick sets the key (ft_1_28 rule 8), a press on the time signature the time panel (time, pick-up, tempo).
   - Listen plays the piece; Try it opens the practice screen on it (no XP, back to the editor). Leaving with unsaved changes asks first.
   - The staff lies on the parchment (d119), moved 12 fine pixels in from the left and right so the torn sides show beside the brace. Clicks, shadow notes and the target box count from the staff's own left side.
8. **Not built:** ft_1_02b to ft_1_02d (files, `private/pieces/`, on and off, dots, piano entry); deleting an own piece from the screen; lit notes during playback; a tablet check. Not verified: how it feels to a parent or the student.

## Acceptance criteria

- **ft_1_02a.AC1** Model and hit testing are unit and property tested (any sequence of edits keeps every note inside its bar and no two notes of a hand overlapping; undo restores the exact previous state; hit testing round-trips with the layout for every staff position and eighth).
- **ft_1_02a.AC10** Writing in order: a shadow note at the tonic shows every place a note can go (the start of an empty bar, after the last note or rest, the start of each gap); a click there writes a note of the selected length, shortened only to fit; the Notes / Rests switch (key 0) writes a rest instead, and a note put on such a rest takes its place; free time is not drawn as rests while writing, while the saved and played piece has its rests. Pieces and files without placed rests load unchanged.
- **ft_1_02a.AC11** A steady staff: while writing, the staff size, the clef and the lines of the first line never change; each bar is as wide as what is written in it (an empty bar one note wide), and bars that do not fit a line go to the next; lines beyond the screen are on further pages (the < and > buttons, Page Up and Page Down). Playing keeps bars of equal width.
- **ft_1_02a.AC12** What an action changes is highlighted: under the pointer a soft box on the hand's staff covers the part of the bar a click, the wheel or Ctrl + wheel would change: over a note or anywhere in the column of a chord, the whole chord for its length; on a placed rest, that rest; on a free place, the time the new note or rest would take (`targetOf`, `targetBox`). Inside a note of that hand, where a click is refused, nothing is highlighted.
- **ft_1_02a.AC2** An editor-written piece compiles and lints the same as the same piece written in ABC.
- **ft_1_02a.AC3** An end-to-end test writes a piece with real clicks and keys, saves it, finds it in the menu, plays it with the virtual player and earns XP.
- **ft_1_02a.AC4** Two or more pitches of one hand can share a start tick and length; each is a separate note in the saved piece. A chord shares one length (lengthening or shortening one note changes all), and a note added where notes already start takes that length.
- **ft_1_02a.AC5** The same pitch twice on one tick of a hand is refused with the reason `duplicate`.
- **ft_1_02a.AC6** A partial overlap (a different start or length) is still refused with `no-room`.
- **ft_1_02a.AC7** The ABC of a chord (`[CEG]2`) compiles to one chart note per pitch, equal to the chart the editor draws, and reads back as the same notes.
- **ft_1_02a.AC8** A piece without chords is written and compiled as before (no schema change).
- **ft_1_02a.AC9** A click on a place selects the note with that start and pitch, and otherwise adds one (a chord note on an occupied start); the decision is a pure helper (`placeClick`).
- **ft_1_02a.AC13** Save (and Export, which saves first) on a new piece whose title is empty or still the one a new piece starts with ("My piece") saves nothing: it puts the typing in the title field of the top strip and says to name the piece; Enter in the title field then saves it under that name (a piece saved without a name seems forgotten). A piece already saved keeps saving under its title, unless its title was emptied (then it asks too). Export on a piece that asks saves and exports nothing until the name is given.
- **ft_1_02a.AC14** The screen without a start screen: a new piece opens on its staff in 4/4 and C; the top strip holds Save after the door, the title field (empty for a new piece, with a placeholder), the page buttons, then Import and Export before the gear; + and - after the last bar add or take four bars; the actions hold Listen, Try it and the circle of fifths with Undo, Redo, Delete, Copy and Paste, no More page; notes and rests are two items, the armed one on purple. A press on the time signature opens and closes the time panel (time 2/4, 3/4, 4/4 or A by arrows or the wheel, while the piece is empty; the pick-up; the tempo); a press on the clef opens the circle of fifths (`tests/e2e/editor.spec.ts`, `tests/e2e/band.spec.ts`).
