# core_09: Notation growth

Index: [FEATURES.md](FEATURES.md).

More of real music, as a beginner's lessons need it. The renderer and the compiler grow together, a step at a time.

## Rules

- **Refused today** (`docs/authoring/au02-abc-subset.md`): double sharps and flats (au02 list; core_09.AC4 names `^^ __`, check au02), meter changes inside a piece, a key change inside a bar, dotted notes other than the dotted half, quarter and eighth, notes shorter than a sixteenth. The schema already allows spelled accidentals, ties, key and meter maps and unequal bars (`docs/futureproofing.md` 1.1). The level table's `chords` switch is on at every level (a level never switches chords off), so bundled pieces may use chords from level 1; pieces written in the editor ignore it.
- **Chords** (`src/core/layout/staff.ts`, `StaffView.ts`): notes of one track that start together and have one length are one column of heads with one stem (direction from the head farthest from the middle line; the stem runs from the near head to an octave beyond the far head). A second puts one head on the other side of the stem. Every head has its own ledger lines; the staff's rooms hold every head and the stem. A chord's note names are one label under it (low to high), only the top head keeps its finger, the cursor box and arrow are one per column, a chord of eighths or sixteenths is beamed with its beat like a single note (core_09.AC40). Refused: chord notes of different lengths, the same pitch twice. In Tempo mode with the Scores view each chord note has its own score, stacked 1.4 staff spaces per note above the chord; the practice screen reserves room for the whole stack (`scoreRoom`), only where scores can show, so Wait mode keeps its bigger notes.
- **Ties** (d101, notation a beginner meets early) compile and are judged as one note (core_01.AC6); drawing, accidental carry, level switch and editor are core_09.AC10 to AC13.
- **Dynamic marks** (d101; a first year of piano teaches *p* and *f*) are shown, not judged: they are notation to read (AX-0), and judging loudness waits for the hardware spike to measure the EZ-220's velocity. Later scoring ideas (a loudness meter, a bonus star, never a penalty) are for later.
- **Repeats** (d102): the chart keeps the written bars once plus a list of repeats; a pure `unrollRepeats` gives the flat play chart the judge and audio understand, and the staff draws the written music with repeat signs while the cursor jumps back (the student must read the sign, AX-0). First and second endings are read too (d269); nested repeats stay refused.
- **Pick-up bars and slurs** (d120, notation found on beginners' sheet music): a pick-up (eeltakt) is a short first bar counted in from a downbeat, numbered 0 as printed; a slur is drawn and graded as legato (held to the next note in Tempo mode, shown in Wait mode).
- **Grace notes** (d121) are built in compiler, staff, judge, playback and editor; core_09.AC31 to AC36.
- **Ornaments and grace groups** ([d305](../decisions/notation.md#d305)): trill, upper and lower mordent and turn signs, and grace groups of up to four, are read, drawn and heard; they are optional for the student, so playing their notes is never wrong and never needed (core_09.AC60 to AC66). The piece maker and the MusicXML reader do not write them yet.
- **Dotted quarters** (the first slice for real classics) are built in compiler, staff, judge and linter; core_09.AC37 to AC39. The eighth after a dotted quarter starts off the beat, so it keeps its flag. The editor does not write them yet.
- **Sixteenths and beams** (d155) are built in staff, judge and linter; core_09.AC40 to AC43. Eighths, dotted eighths and sixteenths are beamed in runs inside one beat (`src/core/layout/beams.ts`), flat, with one stem direction per run; a sixteenth has a second beam, a partial one when it has no sixteenth beside it. AX-3 provisional: beams group by the beat (four eighths in 4/4 are two pairs) and lie flat. Level 5 allows sixteenths and dotted eighths, so imported pieces can be bundled. On the laptop a piece of sixteenths shows one bar a line (core_09.AC44). A draft piece, Little Gallop (level 5), shows them.
- **Key changes** (core_09.AC56, AC57) are built in compiler, staff, linter and the songs: a `K:` line at a bar line, written by every hand; the staff draws the double bar line, the cancelling naturals and the new signature, and a song whose part sets its own key draws its change. The piece maker, the MusicXML and photo importers do not write a key change yet.
- **The piece maker writes them** (core_09.AC55): dotted quarter, dotted eighth and sixteenth buttons beside the others, notes on a sixteenth grid, and a photo or MusicXML import that holds them keeps them.
- **Our own renderer** draws every notation here (d155). Most real songs need some of these notations, so this, not the file format, is the limit of piece import (ft_Y_04).

## Acceptance criteria

- **core_09.AC1** A chord (several notes of one track with the same start and length) is laid out as one column with one stem, seconds beside the stem, ledger lines and rooms for every head, and a piece with chords compiles, passes the level check (own pieces) and is played in Wait mode by pressing every key (`src/core/layout/staff.test.ts`, `tests/editor/ownPieces.test.ts`).
- **core_09.AC2** A chart's key is a structured signature (`fifths` from -7 to 7, `mode` major or minor). A version-1 chart migrates to C major and a version-1 chart with another key is refused (`src/core/chart/migrate.test.ts`, `src/core/chart/key.test.ts`).
- **core_09.AC3** Which accidental to print is a pure rule: a note prints its alter when it differs from the key signature or from an earlier accidental on the same pitch (step and octave) in the same bar, a natural when it cancels one, and nothing otherwise; the bar line forgets accidentals (`src/core/chart/key.test.ts`).
- **core_09.AC4** ABC accidentals (`^ _ = ^^ __`, in chords too) hold for their pitch to the bar line, and `K:` takes any major or minor key, so a piece in G compiles its F as F sharp and its key into the chart (`src/core/piece/abc.test.ts`, `tools/piece/lint.test.ts`). Out-of-key accidentals and keys are limited by the level's `accidentals` and `keys` fields, which every level now sets to allow them all (core_09.AC9).
- **core_09.AC5** The staff draws the key signature after the clef on each staff and system, and accidentals left of the head without overlap, in single notes and chords (layout unit tests in `src/core/layout/staff.test.ts`; a screenshot test).
- **core_09.AC6** A hint label is the spelled name of the note (F#, Bb, from the chart's spelling, not from MIDI), and follows the `noteNaming` setting: in H mode a flat B (Bb) is "B", a natural B is "H", a single sign that lands on a natural reads that natural (B# and H# "C", E# "F", Cb "H", Fb "E"), and A# stays "A#"; the labels of a chord are joined with a slash ("C#/E/G") (`src/core/text/noteName.test.ts`, and the layout's labels in `src/core/layout/staff.test.ts`). The staff's hint labels use this helper with the player's `noteNaming` setting; a long label such as F# on a crowded staff is not yet checked on the screen (needs human).
- **core_09.AC7** Wait and Tempo mode accept either spelling of a black key: a written Db4 and a written C#4 (both MIDI 61) are judged correct by MIDI 61 and wrong by MIDI 60 (`src/core/judge/enharmonic.test.ts`).
- **core_09.AC8** In the editor a parent can set the key (a press on the clef opens the circle of fifths, whose tick sets it), mark a note sharp, flat or natural, save, reopen and play it; an own piece saved before the change opens unchanged (`src/core/editor/key.test.ts`, `src/core/editor/model.test.ts`, `src/core/editor/hitTest.test.ts`, `src/core/editor/pieceFile.test.ts`, `tests/editor/keySignature.test.ts`, `tests/editor/ownPieceFormat.test.ts`).
- **core_09.AC9** Accidentals and key signatures are always available to the linter: no level holds them back (every level in `content/curriculum/levels.json` sets `accidentals: true` and all fifteen `keys`), and two draft pieces, one in G (F sharp in the key, a natural F) and one in F (B flat), lint with no key or accidental error at every level and build (`tools/piece/lint.test.ts`, the bundled-piece test; `content/pieces/g-major-walk`, `content/pieces/f-major-song`). Accidentals are part of the core game, not a level of their own.
- **core_09.AC10** A tie is drawn as a curve from head to head on the side away from the stem, across a bar line, and as two half curves across a line or page break; a tied chord has one curve per head. The tied-to note gets no finger, no hint label and no cursor stop of its own (`src/core/layout/staff.test.ts`; a screenshot test).
- **core_09.AC11** A tie carries its first note's accidental: the tied-to note in the next bar prints none and sounds and is judged at the same pitch, and a later untied note of that pitch in that bar follows core_09.AC3 (`src/core/chart/key.test.ts`, `src/core/piece/abc.test.ts`).
- **core_09.AC12** Every level in `content/curriculum/levels.json` sets `ties: true`, and a draft two-hand piece with whole-note dyads tied across bar lines lints with no tie error at every level and builds (`tools/piece/lint.test.ts`, the bundled-piece test). AX-3 provisional: ties are core notation like accidentals, not a level of their own.
- **core_09.AC13** In the editor a parent can tie a note to the next note of the same pitch (also across a bar line), save, reopen and play it in Wait mode with one press for the chain; an own piece saved before the change opens unchanged (own-piece file `schemaVersion` bump, d050; no profile save bump) (`src/core/editor/model.test.ts`, `src/core/editor/pieceFile.test.ts`).
- **core_09.AC14** A note's `dynamic` is one of `pp p mp mf f ff` and holds from that note on; ABC `!pp!` to `!ff!` before a note or chord compiles to it and any other dynamic is refused with its position; the chart schema refuses another value (`src/core/piece/abc.test.ts`, `src/core/chart/schema.test.ts`).
- **core_09.AC15** The staff draws a dynamic mark as bold italic letters below the note it starts at, between the staves on the grand staff, without overlapping fingers, hint labels or the next mark; while note-name hints are on, the mark's Estonian word (VAIKSELT for p, VALJULT for f, string keys for all six) is shown beside it (`src/core/layout/staff.test.ts`). AX-3 provisional: the word teaches the sign's meaning and goes with the hints, the mark itself is never translated.
- **core_09.AC16** In the editor a parent can set or clear a dynamic mark on the selected note, save and reopen it unchanged; loudness is not judged in any mode (`src/core/editor/model.test.ts`, `src/core/editor/pieceFile.test.ts`).
- **core_09.AC17** ABC `|:`, `:|` and `::` compile to the chart's `repeats` (written bars, played twice; a `:|` with no `|:` repeats from the first bar); first and second endings, nested or overlapping repeats and a tie across a repeat sign are refused with their position. Chart `schemaVersion` 3: a version-2 chart migrates with `repeats: []` (`src/core/piece/abc.test.ts`, `src/core/chart/migrate.test.ts`).
- **core_09.AC18** `unrollRepeats(chart)` is a pure function that returns a flat chart playing each repeated bar range twice, with every note's written note index; a chart with no repeats comes back unchanged; Wait and Tempo mode play an unrolled chart to the end of the second pass (a new repeats test in `src/core/chart/`, `src/core/judge/tempo.test.ts`).
- **core_09.AC19** The staff draws the written chart once with repeat bar lines (dots facing the repeated bars, also at a line start and end), and the practice cursor jumps from the end repeat back to the start repeat on the second pass (`src/core/layout/staff.test.ts`; an end-to-end test with the virtual player).
- **core_09.AC20** In the editor a parent can set a repeat start and end on bar lines (with the band one repeat item: a click in the start bar, then in the end bar, `repeatAround`), save, reopen and play it twice; a section (core_03) that holds a whole repeat keeps it and one that cuts it plays its bars once; per-bar practice records are keyed by the written bar (`src/core/editor/model.test.ts`, `src/core/chart/section.test.ts`). AX-3 provisional: both passes count for the same written bar. AX-3 provisional: the chart and the own-piece file keep `repeats` as an optional field with no `schemaVersion` bump (chart stays 2, piece file stays 2), so older files read unchanged; an older build importing a newer file drops its repeats silently.
- **core_09.AC21** A piece whose first bar is shorter than its meter compiles to a chart whose `bars[0]` holds only those beats (same length in every voice); the last bar then holds exactly the rest of that bar so the two make one whole bar (without a pick-up the last bar is whole), any other short bar is refused with its position as today, and a whole-bar rest `Z` in a pick-up bar is refused. The linter's `bar-length` rule follows the same rule. No chart `schemaVersion` bump (`src/core/piece/abc.test.ts`, `tools/piece/lint.test.ts`) (d120).
- **core_09.AC22** `fullBarTicks(chart)` comes from the meter map and `pickupTicks(chart)` is the first bar's shortfall (0 without a pick-up); the transport, the metronome, the staff layout and the compiler's note bar take the bar length from the meter map (`src/core/chart/time.test.ts`, a pick-up case in `src/core/transport/transport.test.ts`) (d120).
- **core_09.AC23** With a pick-up the count-in starts on a downbeat and lasts the usual full bar(s) plus the beats the pick-up lacks; the metronome accents beat 1 of every full bar; the first note of a one-beat pick-up in 4/4 is due on beat 4 of the count (`src/core/transport/transport.test.ts`, `src/core/judge/tempo.test.ts`) (d120).
- **core_09.AC24** The staff prints clef, key and time signature before the pick-up, gives the pick-up bar the width of its own notes, draws no rests for its missing beats, and the playhead crosses it at the piece's speed (`src/core/layout/staff.test.ts`, `src/core/layout/playhead.test.ts`; a screenshot test) (d120).
- **core_09.AC25** Bar numbers shown to people and the meta file's `phrases[].bars` number the pick-up 0 and the first full bar 1, as a printed score does; a piece with no pick-up keeps its numbers (`src/core/piece/abc.test.ts` or the compile test, the analysis labels of core_10) (d120).
- **core_09.AC26** ABC `(` … `)` compiles to the chart's optional `slurs` (`track`, `voice`, `startTick`, `endTick`); a slur over fewer than two different onsets, a nested or dotted slur, an unclosed one and a slur across a repeat sign are refused with their position; the schema refuses a slur whose ends are not notes of its voice or that overlaps another; `unrollRepeats` copies slurs into each pass; a section (`sliceChart`, as a learning run cuts one) keeps a slur it holds whole, moved, and leaves out one it cuts (`src/core/piece/abc.test.ts`, `src/core/chart/schema.test.ts`, `src/core/chart/repeats.test.ts`, `src/core/chart/section.test.ts`) (d120).
- **core_09.AC27** The staff draws a slur from the first head to the last, on the head side when all its stems point one way and above otherwise, clear of the heads between, and as two halves across a line break; while note-name hints are on, the word LEGAATO (a string key, as VAIKSELT in core_09.AC15) is shown above it (`src/core/layout/staff.test.ts`; a screenshot test) (d120).
- **core_09.AC28** In Tempo mode a slurred note other than the last scores a full hold only when held until the next note's onset (released at most 30 ms early; overlap never lowers it), through the d093 hold factor; it is never a miss and never takes XP away; with "Grade note length" off nothing changes (`src/core/judge/tempo.test.ts`) (d120). AX-3 provisional: 30 ms.
- **core_09.AC29** In Wait mode, when the next note under a slur is pressed, the judge reports whether the previous slurred key was still held, and the staff shows it as objective feedback on the slur; the score does not change (`src/core/judge/wait.test.ts` or its neighbour, `src/core/layout/staff.test.ts`) (d120). AX-3 provisional: feedback only.
- **core_09.AC30** In the editor a parent can set a pick-up length and draw a slur from one note to a later note of the same hand, save, reopen and play it; an own piece saved before the change opens unchanged (`src/core/editor/model.test.ts`, `src/core/editor/pieceFile.test.ts`) (d120). The pick-up is set on the time panel (a press on the time signature), from none to one beat less than the bar; a written first bar keeps its notes where they sound, so a first bar whose notes are in its last beats becomes a pick-up, refused only where a note or the last bar's notes would not fit: bar 0 then holds only those beats and the last bar the rest, the chart the editor draws equals the compiled one, the pick-up is numbered 0, a MusicXML or photo import whose short first bar pairs with its last bar opens with it, and bars cut or pasted keep every note in its bar (an optional `pickup` field, no save bump; `src/core/editor/pickup.test.ts`, the editor end-to-end test).
- **core_09.AC31** ABC `{g}C` and `{/g}C` compile to the chart's optional `graces` (`track`, `voice`, `tick`, `pitch`, `slash`); two notes in the braces, a grace before a rest or a chord, a grace with the main note's pitch and a second grace on one note are refused with their position; the schema refuses a grace whose `tick` is not a note of its voice; `unrollRepeats` copies graces into each pass; the notes, bars and beats of the chart are the same with and without them (`src/core/piece/abc.test.ts`, `src/core/chart/schema.test.ts`, `src/core/chart/repeats.test.ts`) (d121).
- **core_09.AC32** The staff draws a grace head small, left of its main note, with a stem, a flag and a slash when `slash` is set, with its own ledger lines and accidental, clear of the previous note, accidental and tie, at every zoom and on a line's first note; note-name hints show its letter small (`src/core/layout/staff.test.ts`, positions asserted; `tests/e2e/grace-notes.spec.ts`, the drawn head, stem and slash read back from the canvas; a screenshot test) (d121).
- **core_09.AC33** A press of a grace note's pitch before its main note (Wait mode: while that note is awaited; Tempo mode: up to 250 ms before the onset) is ignored by the judge: no wrong note, no score, no XP change; a grace never needs a press, and its release leaves the hold of other notes alone; the key-bash guard (ft_1_15) counts the grace's key as one more held, so the main note after it never waits (`src/core/judge/keyBash.test.ts`, `src/core/judge/wait.test.ts`, `src/core/judge/tempo.test.ts`, `src/core/judge/tempo.hold.test.ts`; `tests/e2e/grace-notes.spec.ts` plays Grace Steps in Wait mode with the virtual EZ-220, grace keys included) (d121). AX-3 provisional: 250 ms (`GRACE_EARLY_MS`). The judge events are unchanged: an ignored press emits none, so the event version stays 2.
- **core_09.AC34** Computer playback sounds each grace 60 ms before its main onset, ending at the onset, and leaves the main note's time unchanged. When the piece's first note has a grace, the Tempo-mode count-in is the bar's ticks with the grace in place of the last tick (4/4: tick, tick, tick, grace, bar 1); the metronome leaves that beat silent and the piano sounds the grace from there to the first note, handed over only within the metronome's 120 ms lookahead so a screen restarted during the count-in leaves no grace behind. AX-3 provisional (`src/core/chart/playback.test.ts`, `src/game/practice/countInGrace.test.ts`, `src/core/transport/transport.test.ts`, `src/io/audio/Metronome.test.ts`) (d121).
- **core_09.AC35** In the editor a note can carry one pre-note (`grace` on the editor note: letter, octave, alter, slash). The GRACE NOTE item (EELNOOT; in the action and inventory band and the piece maker's tool row beside the slurs) arms it on the selected note; the next pitch picked (staff click, EZ-220, on-screen piano or QWERTY) becomes the slashed grace instead of a new note; the same item on a note that has one removes it. A chord, the second note of a tie, the note's own pitch and keys off the keyboard are refused with a reason; a pre-note a later edit made unwritable is kept but not written. Undo and redo work. The ABC the editor writes holds `{/g}` (`{g}` when not slashed) with the accidental held to the bar line and compiles back to the same notes and graces (the property test of editor chart against compiled ABC includes graces and ties) (`src/core/editor/graces.test.ts`, `tests/editor/abc.test.ts`, `tests/e2e/editor.spec.ts`) (d121).
