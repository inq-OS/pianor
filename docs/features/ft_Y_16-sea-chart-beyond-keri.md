# ft_Y_16: The sea chart

Later pool. The close-ups of Keri ([ft_1_38](ft_1_38-close-ups-on-keri.md)) end at the shore; the boat built in the boat shed takes the student further. Decision: [d302](../decisions/game.md#d302). Index: [FEATURES.md](FEATURES.md).

AX-0: a crossing moves only when a note read from the staff is played right; a crossing the student only watches is not allowed. AX-1: return trips start faster. AX-2: every mark on the chart comes from play.

## The vision

1. **The chart is the curriculum.** A painted chart in the manner of the 1991 island maps, reached from the edge of Keri's map or the warden's chart on the room's wall. A route between two islands is a piece; its buoys are its bars, lit one by one as the piece is learned bar by bar (ft_1_32); a route is drawn in once learned and inked bold once proficient (ft_1_13). The teacher's curriculum (ft_1_26) may put any piece on any route, so an island opens places, never pieces.
2. **Crossings are played.** Rowing: one stroke per right note in Wait mode, one per beat in Tempo mode (core_01). Riding with the fisherman: he leads a hauling line, the student answers phrase by phrase (ft_1_21). Sailing: the sail fills only while a note is held its full value (core_01 slice f, ft_1_11). Every new crossing of a learned route starts a little faster (core_03), and the chart shows the route's best speed. Fog routes are crossed by ear (ft_1_10), the answer then shown on the staff.
3. **Each islet is a region of one skill** (`AX-3 provisional:`; places and facts to verify with the maintainer): Prangli the folk songs and the left hand joining; Aksi rhythm; Rammu the bass clef; Pedassaar meters and dances; Kräsuli the black keys and the first key signatures; Vaindloo, Keri's sister light, the minor keys, with a duet where the two lights answer each other on the grand staff; the Kagusäär spit ledger lines; Porkkala a long whole piece. The Viimsi coast is where the teacher's boat comes from.
4. **How regions open:** by player level, ear level and pieces learned, all read from the logs.

## First step

A chart with only Keri and Prangli, one route (the fisherman's first tune) and rowing in Wait mode; Prangli is an arrival picture until the maintainer gives its photos and word.
