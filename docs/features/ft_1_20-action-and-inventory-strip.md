# ft_1_20: Actions in a bubble, a pinned strip and a backpack

The band under every screen goes ([d282](../decisions/game.md#d282)): it took a third of the screen to show a few actions, most of them seldom used, and its empty slots on every place. In its place, three things, each where the hand already is:

- **The bubble.** A right click opens a ring of what can be done with the thing under the pointer, centred on the pointer so the hand moves least; on practice a right click anywhere opens it (no dead spot). Each option has a fixed slot (slot 0 straight up, then clockwise, two cells at least between neighbours), so the hand learns where it is, and shows its current value (Wait, 80%, lit when on). An option with choices opens them on a second, outer ring round the bubble, centred on that option (turned a step or two, a long row as far as it still reaches its option, or the bubble moved out from the edge, where the screen has no room); neither the choices nor, where the bubble moved for them, an option's plate lie over the thing the bubble was opened on (its note stays in sight), and the bubble moves on away from it where a corner leaves no other room. Every plate and the choices' sausage, not only the boxes a press counts in, stay whole on the screen, and the bubble never lies over the strip. A screen may name what the choices should leave in sight (`avoid`; the piece maker names its staff's systems), and the ring turns toward the empty side where a turn can; while the piece maker's bubble is open its message strip waits, shown again when the bubble closes. AX-3 provisional: covering the thing pressed counts as 32 cells of moving the bubble (`COVER_COST`), each choice over a kept place 3 (`AVOID_COST`). TODO(architect, R2): confirm the cost. A pick does it and closes the bubble; a worn option (note names, fingers) switches and leaves it open, so several can be set in a row. The centre is a Close target; Esc, a right click again or a press outside closes it too, and the press that closes is swallowed, never also hitting what is under it. A slot fires on press and release on the same slot. A piano key closes the bubble and still counts as played. Each option shows its keyboard key in its corner, so the bubble teaches the shortcuts; its name is in a tooltip bubble. Code: `bubbleLayout`, `bubblePage` in `src/core/layout/bubble.ts`.
- **Touch and keyboard.** A long press opens the bubble at 450 ms, with a ring filling round the finger from 150 ms; moving more than 12 screen pixels or lifting cancels; the lift after the long press never picks. Two fingers that land within 200 ms of each other (a palm-sized contact does not count) open the bubble at once, midway between them, and their lift picks nothing; two fingers that then slide more than 24 pixels are a pinch and close it. A finger tap on a bar of practice acts on its lift, so a long press or a two-finger tap never restarts the screen under the fingers. On touch a finger down on a slot shows its name, the lift fires it, sliding off cancels. Shift + F10 and the context-menu key open the bubble at the keyboard focus; the arrows walk the slots clockwise, Enter picks, Esc closes and gives the focus back. Every cell and slot shows the focus ring.
- **The strip.** A screen pins its most-used actions, and the ones whose state must stay visible, in one slim row along its foot (17 rows, cells 30 x 15, at most eight cells): left to right in a fixed order, the screen's main action (Play again, Done) at the right end in the same place on every screen, a destructive one (Delete) away from it at the left. A screen with nothing pinned has no strip and its main area takes every row. A screen with a bubble ends its left group with More (the bubble's own ring as its picture, its word VEEL when it fits, in the same place whatever is pinned), which opens the same bubble over the strip, so a student who never right-clicks, a touch screen and the keyboard all reach every action; it pulses gently on a player's first three visits of the screen. Right of More, the worn options that are on and not the default (a section, note names, fingers, the click) stand as small lit pictures; a press on one turns it off. A word is never cut short: a word wider than its cell takes as many cells as it needs (`cellSpan`), and a pinned action that does not fit goes into the bubble (`pinFlow`).
- **The backpack.** The items a student keeps (a character's prize, ft_1_21.AC15) are in a backpack, a picture in the top right corner two cells left of the gear on the room, the map and the scenes, there from the start; a new item makes it bounce once and wear a dot until it is opened, and Kiur says so once. A press opens it: the backpack itself grows from its corner and opens into a larger open bag (the small one is not shown beside it), its items lying inside in 3 x 3 places with no box round them, scrolled by the wheel, a drag or the side strap's arrows when there are more; a second press or Esc closes it. No screen shows empty slots.
- **Teaching it.** On a player's first practice, Kiur points at More and says a press and hold (or a right click) on the staff opens it, with a picture of the mouse's right button and of a finger, and stops after the first time the bubble opens; it is kept with the first-run intro's flags (ft_1_31).

**The look** (`src/game/ui/pixel/bubbleArt.ts`, in layout cells, sized to `bubble.ts`): an option is a round plate 24 across (`optionPlate`, `OPTION_PLATE`) centred on its 20-cell box, so neighbouring plates may overlap a little while the boxes where a press counts never touch; the bubble has no dark outline and nothing behind the ring, and is drawn 85% solid (`BUBBLE_ALPHA`), so the staff shows between and faintly through the plates; the Close target is a sunk round plate with a cross (`closePlate`); an option's choices lie in one round-ended sausage curved along the outer ring (`choicesSausage`, given the bubble's centre and the layout's choices, it says where it is drawn), not a plate each, the choice in use on a gold-rimmed patch and a greyed one (and a greyed option) on a sunk dark patch, its picture paler (a greyed choice also struck through corner to corner); the dynamic of a note with none shows the dashed empty picture (its none choice too, named in its tooltip), the finger written on the note is lit among the fingers, and AUTO is the fingers' picture (the word is wider than a choice); a plate has seven looks: plain, hover, down, on (a gold edge), off, main (a honey edge, the strip's main action) and armed (purple, the piece maker's writer while a click writes with it); the keyboard's focus on an option is a gold ring (`optionFocus`); a long press fills a gold ring round the finger (`pressRing`); the strip's ground (`stripBitmap`) and its cells (`stripCell`, `pinCell` for a span of cells joined); a worn option that is on is a gold chip with its picture in ink (`wornChip`); the backpack (`BACKPACK`, 14 x 15), its new-item dot (`NEW_ITEM_DOT`) and the open bag (`openBackpack`, 112 x 106, with its 3 x 3 places `openBagPlaces` and the scroll arrows). More's picture is the ring (`TOOL_ICONS.more`); a picture in a bubble's plate is at most 14 wide (the section's is `sectionShort`). `npm run art:render -- ui <practice|note|scene|kit>` shows them at 1920 x 1080. AX-3 provisional: the colours.

A screen whose actions all fit the strip and has nothing to point at pins them all and has no bubble (a short list a child sees beats a hidden one, AX-0, AX-2.4). The bubble is for the two screens with many actions on things: practice and the piece maker.

## Rules

Axioms: the strip gives the staff room and the bubble covers it only while open, see-through (AX-0); neither adds a reward or a sound (AX-2); cells and options are at least 15 rows high, text is the game's font in Estonian capitals, one press does one thing, and there is no verb-and-object grammar on places (AX-2.4).

### The strip, the bubble and the backpack (layout cells, d110; smallest screen 320 x 180)

Code: `src/core/layout/bubble.ts` (`bubbleLayout`, `bubblePage`, `pinRows`, `pinFlow`, `cellSpan`, `wheelChoice`); `PixelScene` draws them. A screen declares `stripActions()` (pinned, left to right; the one marked `main` stands at the right end), `bubbleOptions(at)` (slot by slot for what is under the pointer, or null for no bubble) and `backpackItems()`.

1. **The strip** is one row of 30 x 15 cells along the foot (17 rows; none on a screen with nothing pinned). A word takes the cells it needs and is never cut short; what does not fit goes into the bubble. More (the ring of dots, its word in the tooltip) ends the left group on a screen with a bubble and pulses on its first three visits; a worn option that is on stands lit beside it.
2. **The bubble** opens round the pointer on a right click, a long press on a touch screen (450 ms, a ring fills from 150 ms; more than 12 pixels of movement cancels it) or a two-finger tap (midway between the fingers), Shift + F10 or the context-menu key at the keyboard focus, or More (then over More, on the strip). Eight round options sit in fixed slots on a ring of radius 28 (slot 0 up, then clockwise), Close in the centre; the ground is see-through, so the staff shows under it. A press anywhere in an option's slice of the ring picks it. An option with choices opens them on an outer ring of radius 56, centred on the option, 26 cells apart along it; an option that sets a value in a range opens a slider there instead, a track along the outer ring where five choices would stand (its least first), whose knob a press or a drag on the track moves, showing each value as it goes and keeping the one let go of, and the left and right arrows move a step and keep it (`sliderArc`, `sliderFraction`, `sliderStep`); a worn option switches and leaves the bubble open. The press that closes the bubble reaches nothing under it, Esc closes it, and on practice a piano key closes it and still counts. The bubble keeps below the top strip of a screen without a toolbar.
3. **Global actions** stay in place: **Back** (door) top-left, **Settings** (gear) top-right, drawn over the main area (`frameCorners`), each the toolbar's own boxed button on its wooden plate, solid at all times and of the same size and hit area as on the toolbar: Back is one look on every screen (the teacher's toolbar too), never a bare picture, so it is not taken for a painted door and is not lost over a book or a picture. Left of the gear: the piano's status picture on practice (lit while a piano is connected, what it means in its tooltip), and the **backpack** on the room, the map and the scenes (the items the student keeps; a press opens them below it).
4. **A painted place is used in its picture.** The room's piano, door, note board and gramophone (ft_1_08.AC7) and a scene's places and characters are pressed where painted. A thing shows it is pressable while the pointer is on it (AC14, AC15); keyboard focus walks them in fixed order (ft_Y_13). A thing answers in its box, larger than its painted shape (AX-3 provisional; a true-shape hit area only where boxes overlap).

Practice and the piece maker: no bars; the staff gets every row above the strip. Kiur and his bubble stand in the strip between the door and the gear and end short of the pictures left of the gear; with the strip the staff starts under that top strip, and what stood beside the staff is drawn over it (`overStaff()`). Kiur says once, on a player's first practice that is not the intro, learning or a check-up, that the bubble is there (AX-3 provisional: kept per player in the browser). The piece maker's right button opens the bubble and does not remove (Delete is in the bubble). The desk screens fit their rows (d130); every title starts right of the door (`PixelScene.titleX`).

A `ToolbarItem` may be `disabled`, keep its case, be `main` or `worn`, open choices (`menu`) and hand back its button (`ref`). End-to-end tests find a pinned action or a bubble option with `bandAction` and `sceneWords` in `tests/e2e/helpers.ts`.

### Held marks (an example)

A held mark is put on a note by clicking it (a tie: the first note, then the one it goes to; a dynamic on the note under the pointer). With a note selected, pressing an item applies it at once, so select-then-hotkey keeps working. Each item names its target kind (`note`, `bar`, `place on the staff`, `piece`), so a later mark (repeat signs, dots and triplets, dynamics between staves) is one new entry. The item model is pure (`src/core/`): id, kind, target, string key, picture key, and for a held mark the edit it makes through existing core functions (`core/editor/ties.ts`, dynamics and accidental edits). The scene keeps which item is held. Built so far: the items a student wins (`content/items/items.json`, `core/item/item.ts`: id, kind `kept`, name key, picture key; ft_1_21.AC15). Not built: the held, worn and opening kinds, a held item's target and held items riding on the pointer (AC4), focus by cell index (AC7).

### The room

`ProfileDashboardScene`: the name is written on the rank shield, whole when it fits its band (about six letters) and otherwise whole on a blue ribbon hung over the shield's point, never cut short (`nameRibbon`, `rankArt.ts`; AX-3 provisional: the ribbon); the shield opens the player's card (`core/progression/playerStats.ts`). The note plant on the piano (`PLANT_STAGES` in `keri/roomLife.ts`) replaces an XP bar. Pressable groups are named in `PRESSABLE_GROUPS` (`keri/roomSet.ts`); `RoomPainter.hover` (`keri/roomPicture.ts`) draws the shield's contour. An overlay (Kiur at the door) keeps the plain frame.
