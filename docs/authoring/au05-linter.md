# au05: Linter rules (`npm run piece:lint`)

Part of [piece authoring](AUTHORING.md).

`npm run piece:lint [piecesDir] [--levels=path] [--strict]` compiles every piece and checks it against the level named in its meta file. Each piece prints one line (level, difficulty, and every level it would pass), then its errors, then a separate list of warnings. Messages read `folder/piece.abc:LINE:COLUMN error: bar 2, beat 3: ... [rule]` and point at the note in the ABC file. At most 8 reports per rule are shown, then a count of the rest. Exit code 1 for any error (or any warning with `--strict`); warnings alone exit 0.

Hard errors (a piece fails; the rule code is in brackets):
- Each bar's total duration equals the meter; no incomplete bars except an optional pickup.
- All pitches inside the level's allowed range and note set; note values only from the level's rhythm set.
- Maximum melodic leap per level (levels 1 and 2: repeated note or a step only; thirds from level 3), read from `levels.json`.
- Bars per phrase between 2 and 8; whole piece 4 to 32 bars at every level (raised from 16 at levels 1-3, so a character's song of up to 32 bars fits a beginner level).
- Fingering, if present, is consistent with the hand position (RH C position: C1 D2 E3 F4 G5; LH C position runs the other way: C5 D4 E3 F2 G1, defined per hand in `levels.json`) and has no impossible jumps.
- Meta file validates against its schema; `source` and `license` present.

Implemented rule codes: `meter`, `hand`, `clef`, `bar-length` (every voice's bars are filled exactly; guards charts that did not come from the compiler), `range`, `note-value` (the dotted half and dotted quarter are named values; other dotted and shorter notes are refused), `accidental`, `tie`, `chord`, `voices` (a second voice in one hand above the level's `voices`, d164), `ornament` (an ornament sign where the level's `ornaments` switch is off, d303), `rest` and `rest-value` (a whole-bar rest is always fine where rests are), `leap` (between consecutive notes of one voice, in diatonic steps, named as an interval), `hands-together` (any overlap of a right-hand and a left-hand note where the level does not allow it), `piece-length`, `phrase-length` (2 to 8 bars) and `fingering` (must equal the level's hand position for that note; the one exception, a warning instead: in a part with a note outside the position, a different finger that the fingering search of ft_1_30 takes on that note too, other written fingers kept, such as the thumb turning under on F; fingers in one hand's chord must differ and follow the hand: right hand higher key, higher finger; left hand the other way).

Warnings (human review): `no-cadence` (a played part does not end on C), `identical-bars` (3 or more identical bars in a row), `repeated-bars` (at most half of the bars are different), `monotone-rhythm` (8 or more notes, all one length), `no-phrases`, `difficulty` (the meta file's confirmed `difficulty` is 2 or more half stars from the estimator's proposal, core_08), `fingering-unchecked` (a fingered note the level has no hand position for, once per note), `fingering-cost` (a written finger the fingering search of ft_1_30 finds 8 or more points harder for the hand than another finger there, all other written fingers kept; it names the better finger), `license` (not one of CC0-1.0, CC-BY-4.0, CC-BY-3.0, CC-BY-SA-4.0, CC-BY-SA-3.0, public-domain; the licence `NaN` of own pieces written in the editor is accepted without a warning in `private/pieces/` and warned about anywhere else, because own pieces are never committed or deployed; `npm run piece:lint private/pieces` lints them).

The linter also outputs a **difficulty**: the estimator's proposal in points 1 to 100 and half stars 1 to 50 ([d157](../decisions/game.md#d157); 1 to 10 is the Beginner grade, the steps of [d138](../decisions/game.md#d138)), its score and the hardest bars as printed ([core_08](../features/core_08-difficulty-estimator.md), `src/core/piece/difficulty.ts`), and lists the levels a piece **fits** (passes without errors). A person confirms the half stars by writing them as `difficulty` in the meta file. Each bar is scored from what the student plays there: onsets per second at the written tempo, rhythm (different lengths, a dotted or tuplet length weighing more than a plain one), leaps beyond a step (the jump into the bar included), five-finger position shifts per hand, accidentals outside the key, chord notes, both hands sounding together and a second voice in one hand. The piece is the bars' mean weighted by their presses, plus half the gap to the hardest bar, plus changes of meter, key and tempo, the key signature, the range beyond five fingers, both clefs and the length. The weights are a first guess (AX-3 provisional).

Songs: after the pieces, the linter checks every song in `content/songs/` beside the pieces folder against the pieces that compiled ([au11](au11-songs.md)).
