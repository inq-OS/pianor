# au02: The ABC subset and the compiler

Part of [piece authoring](AUTHORING.md).

`npm run piece:build [piecesDir] [outDir]` (defaults `content/pieces` and `build/charts`) compiles every `<id>/piece.abc` with its `piece.meta.json`. The folder name must equal the piece `id`. Errors are printed as `folder/piece.abc:LINE:COLUMN error: message`, the piece is not written, other pieces still build, and the exit code is 1. Warnings (a missing `Q:` tempo, a `T:` that differs from the meta title) never stop a build.

**Supported:** fields `X T C M L Q K V` (others that carry no music are ignored); meters 4/4, 3/4, 2/4 and `C`; one key for the whole piece in `K:`, any major or minor key (`C`, `G`, `F#m`, `Bb`, `Dmin`; modes such as `Dor` are refused); accidentals `^` sharp, `^^` double sharp, `_` flat, `__` double flat and `=` natural before a note or inside a chord, which hold for that pitch to the bar line (ties are not drawn yet and a tie does not carry them on); `Q:1/4=70` or `Q:70`; default note length from `L:` (the ABC default when missing); notes `A-G`, `a-g`, octave marks `,` and `'` (`C` is middle C); lengths `2`, `/2`, `3/2`, `/`, `//` (a dotted quarter is `3/2` with `L:1/4` or `3` with `L:1/8`, a sixteenth `/4` and a dotted eighth `3/4`; the staff draws whole to sixteenth notes and rests, dotted halves, quarters and eighths, and beams eighths and sixteenths per beat, core_09.AC37 and AC40 to AC41); rests `z` and whole-bar rests `Z` (`Z2` for two bars); bar lines `| || |] [|`; fingerings `!1!` to `!5!` before a note; chords `[CEG]4` (fingerings inside the brackets, length after); ties `-`; slurs `(` before the first note and `)` after the last, at least two different notes under it (a curve over one pitch is a tie, so `(C-C)` is refused), which may cross bar lines and start on a chord (core_09.AC26; the staff's curve is core_09.AC27); comments `%`.

**Voices:** a piece without `V:` lines is one voice for the single hand in the meta `hands`. With two hands write `V:RH clef=treble`, `V:LH clef=bass` in the header, then select each voice with `V:RH` or `V:LH` before its music. Voice ids are `RH`, `LH` (a hand listed in the meta `hands` is a play track, otherwise accompaniment the computer plays) and `ACC` (no hand). `clef=` is `treble` or `bass`; the default is bass for `LH`. All voices must have the same number of bars.

**Two voices in one hand (d164):** `&` inside a bar starts voice 2 of the same hand at that bar's bar line, up to the next bar line: `E F G F & C4` is a tune over a held C. Voice 2 holds as much as voice 1 in its bar (write its rests with `z`); a bar without `&` has no voice 2. Voice 2 has its own ties, slurs and accidentals (a sharp in voice 1 does not sharpen voice 2), and a voice-2 tie needs `&` in the next bar too. Refused with their position: a second `&` in one bar, `(&` and `&)`, a voice-2 note past the bar line, a `V:` line before voice 2 reaches its bar line, and a pitch one voice strikes while the other starts or still holds it (one key cannot be held and struck again). `Z & C4` rests voice 1 for the bar under voice 2. The staff draws voice 1 stems up and voice 2 stems down; the linter allows two voices from level 5 (`voices` in `levels.json`).

**Chords on the staff:** the staff draws a chord of one hand when its notes start together and have one length (`core_09`); the level switch `chords` is on at every level in `levels.json` (au04: nothing is off), so a piece may use chords from level 1.

**Triplets ([d163](../decisions/notation.md#d163), core_09.AC49):** `(3` before three notes, chords or rests of one length, eighths or quarters, makes them a triplet: three in the time of two, so an eighth triplet fills one beat (160 ticks each) and a quarter triplet two (320 each). It starts on a beat (in a pick-up counted from the bar's end) and stays in one bar. Refused with its position: other tuplets (`(2`, `(4` and up, `(p:q:r`), mixed lengths, other lengths, a triplet inside a triplet, one across a bar line, one off the beat, a whole-bar rest `Z` in it, and one with fewer than three notes after its `(3`. The chart keeps it as `tuplets`; the staff draws the notes at their written value, beamed with a small 3 or under a bracket with a 3 (core_09.AC50); the linter allows it where the level's `tuplets` switch is on (core_09.AC51).

**Grace notes (d121):** one grace note before a note, `{g}C` (plain) or `{/g}C` (slashed, the acciaccatura); the letters inside the braces are notes, so `g` is G5 and `{^F}G` is a sharp F before G. It has no length and takes no bar time. Refused with its position: two notes in the braces, a grace before a rest or a chord, on the second note of a tie, with no note after it (before a bar line, say), with the main note's own pitch, a second grace on one note, an unclosed brace, and a length inside the braces. The chart keeps it as `graces`; the staff draws it small (core_09.AC32), computer playback sounds it 60 ms before its main note, and a first note's grace takes the count-in's last beat (core_09.AC34); the judge never needs it and never counts it wrong (core_09.AC33).

**Refused with a clear message** (never silently accepted): an accidental with no note after it, repeats, tuplets other than triplets, nested slurs (`((`), dotted slurs (`.(`), a slur that is not closed or has no `(`, a slur with no note after its `(`, a slur across a repeat sign, chord symbols and text, other decorations, an overlay over several bars (`(&` `&)`), meter, key, tempo or default-length changes inside the piece, a short bar other than a pick-up and the last bar that completes it, notes that run past a bar line (use a tie), ties between different pitches, and any other character. Length rules per level (note values, range, leaps) are the linter's job (ft_0_04); the compiler only refuses what cannot be a valid chart. Pick-up (d120): a short first bar is a pick-up, the same length in every voice (write its rests with their length, `z`, never `Z`), and the last bar must then hold exactly the beats the pick-up lacks, so the two make one bar. The game counts a pick-up in from a downbeat (a one-beat pick-up in 4/4 is the fourth beat of the count), draws it as wide as its own beats and prints it as bar 0 (core_09.AC23 to AC25); `step-right-in` is the example. Bar and phrase numbers: `phrases[].bars` in the meta file are inclusive and numbered as printed: from 1, or from 0 (the pick-up) when the piece has one; the chart is 0-based.
