What is here: 21 MP3 files, one every minor third from C2 to C7 (C, D#, F#, A in each octave, plus C7),
taken unchanged from the copy hosted by the Tone.js project at
https://github.com/Tonejs/audio/tree/master/salamander (original README says "Licence: CC-by").
The game plays other notes by changing the playback speed of the nearest file.
Original project info: Salamander Grand Piano V2/V3, 48 kHz 24 bit originals, 16 velocity layers; these MP3s are the single-layer web versions.
